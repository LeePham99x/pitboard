A Claude Desktop label starts with `desktop/`, as in `desktop/work`. In the app, choose **Claude Desktop** as the tool and enter the name alone.

## Before you add a Claude Desktop account

- Pitboard switches Claude Desktop on macOS only.
- Pitboard changes Claude's login only while Claude is closed. Quit Claude with Command-Q: closing its window leaves it running.
- [Claude Desktop accounts](/concepts/limits#claude-desktop-accounts) says how these accounts differ from Claude Code's.

<Warning>
  Do not choose **Log out** in Claude: it ends the login Claude holds at claude.ai, and Pitboard cannot bring it back.
</Warning>

## Add the account Claude is signed in to

Open Claude and check which account it shows. Pitboard cannot ask claude.ai whose a login is.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    Click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose **Add Account**. Choose **Claude Desktop** in the **Tool** pop-up menu, enter a name such as `work`, then click **Add Account**. If Claude is open, click **Quit Claude and Add**: Pitboard quits Claude, records the account and opens Claude again.

    If the dialog says Claude Desktop is not offered, open Claude once, then quit Pitboard and open it again. The app looks for Claude's data folder only when it opens.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    Quit Claude, then run:

    ```sh
    pitboard enroll desktop/work
    ```

    ```text
    Enrolled desktop/work, the account Claude is signed in to now. Check that Claude shows that account: Pitboard cannot ask claude.ai whose it is.
    Add another: pitboard enroll desktop/<label> --sign-in
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If Claude has not finished signing in, Pitboard enrols nothing. Open Claude, wait until it shows your chats, quit it, then try again. If Claude is signed out, add the account as the next section shows.

## Add another account

Pitboard parks the account in use, which leaves Claude signed out and signs nothing out at claude.ai. You sign in to the other account in Claude, and Pitboard adds it once Claude quits.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the dialog">
        Click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose **Add Account**. Choose **Claude Desktop** in the **Tool** pop-up menu, then enter a name such as `travel`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Park the account in use">
        Click **Park work**, or **Start Sign-In** while Claude is signed out. If Claude is open, click **Quit Claude and Park work**: Pitboard quits Claude, parks `work` and opens Claude again.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Open Claude if it is closed, and sign in to the account you are adding. When it shows your chats, quit Claude with Command-Q. The dialog closes once Pitboard has added the account.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    Quit Claude, then run:

    ```sh
    pitboard enroll desktop/travel --sign-in
    ```

    Open Claude, sign in to the account you are adding, and quit Claude with Command-Q once it shows your chats.

    ```text
    Parked `desktop/work`. Claude is signed out, and nothing was signed out at claude.ai.
    Open Claude and sign in to the account to add as `desktop/travel`. When Claude shows your chats, quit it with Command-Q.
    Do not choose Log out in Claude: it ends the login Claude holds at claude.ai, and Pitboard cannot bring it back.
    Waiting for Claude to quit. Control-C stops waiting and keeps `desktop/work` parked; `pitboard use desktop/work` puts it back.
    Claude is open.
    Enrolled desktop/travel, the account Claude is signed in to now.
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If Claude quits before the sign-in has finished, Pitboard says so and keeps waiting. If Claude is signed in to an account you have already added, Pitboard adds nothing and names the command to run.

Pitboard refuses to park an account it has not enrolled. Add that account first, as [Add the account Claude is signed in to](#add-the-account-claude-is-signed-in-to) shows.

## Switch accounts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    Click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose the Claude Desktop account. If Claude is open, click **Quit Claude and Switch**: Pitboard quits Claude, switches, and opens Claude again behind the Pitboard window.

    If Claude has not quit within 30 seconds, nothing changes.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    Quit Claude, then run:

    ```sh
    pitboard use desktop/home
    ```

    ```text
    Switched to desktop/home; desktop/work is parked.
    Claude opens signed in to it.
    ```

    While Claude is open, `pitboard use` changes nothing and says `Claude is open`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If an error says part of Claude is still running, see [Claude is open](/troubleshooting#claude-is-open).

## Sign in again

Sign in again when an account shows **Needs signing in again** or `nothing parked`, or Claude asks you to sign in after a switch.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the dialog">
        In the Pitboard window, Control-click `home` and choose **Sign In Again**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Park the account in use">
        Click **Park work**, or **Start Sign-In** while Claude is signed out. If Claude is open, click **Quit Claude and Park work**: Pitboard quits Claude, parks `work` and opens Claude again.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Open Claude if it is closed, and sign in to `home`. When it shows your chats, quit Claude with Command-Q.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    Quit Claude, then run:

    ```sh
    pitboard enroll desktop/home --sign-in
    ```

    Sign in to `home` in Claude, then quit Claude with Command-Q.

    ```text
    Signed in to desktop/home again. Claude is signed in to it now.
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Where the account had a parked login, Pitboard sets it aside and deletes nothing. If Claude is signed in to another account when it quits, Pitboard records nothing for `home` and says why.

## Finish or undo an add

An add left halfway keeps Claude signed out and the account in use parked, after a restart too. You leave one with Control-C on the command line, or **Later** in the app. The app then shows **Adding a Claude Desktop account isn't finished**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    To finish, sign in to the account in Claude and quit Claude. Then click **Finish Adding** on the notice, and **Add the Account Claude Shows** in the dialog. To undo it, click **Put work Back**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    To finish, sign in to the account in Claude, quit Claude, then run `pitboard enroll desktop/travel`. To undo it, run `pitboard use desktop/work`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once Claude is signed in to the new account, the undo is refused. Finish the add instead.

## Give up an interrupted move

Give up only when Pitboard says it cannot tell where an interrupted move stopped. [A Claude Desktop move was interrupted](/troubleshooting/recovery#a-claude-desktop-move-was-interrupted) says what comes first.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    Click **Give Up** on the notice **An interrupted move is waiting**, then **Give Up** again to confirm.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    ```sh
    pitboard abandon
    ```

    ```text
    Pitboard gave up the move of `desktop/work`'s login and kept every folder: 1 parked, 1 set aside. Run `pitboard enroll desktop/<label>` for the account Claude shows, or `pitboard use desktop/<label>` where Claude is signed out.
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Then open Claude, check which account it shows and quit it. Add that account, or switch to an account if Claude is signed out.
