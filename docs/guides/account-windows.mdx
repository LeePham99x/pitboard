The app opens account windows on macOS and Windows, and Linux has no app. Sending a page from your browser to an account works on macOS only, because Windows lists only a signed Pitboard in its share sheet.

Each window keeps its own website data, so your accounts stay signed in side by side. A Claude Code account's window is on claude.ai, a Codex account's on chatgpt.com. [How account windows keep their data](/concepts/account-windows) says where it is and when Pitboard deletes it.

## Open an account window

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    In Pitboard's menu in the menu bar, choose **Open claude.ai as work**. When the site has several accounts, choose **Open claude.ai**, then the account. In the Pitboard window, Control-click an account and choose **Open claude.ai** or **Open chatgpt.com**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    Right-click Pitboard's icon in the notification area and select **Open claude.ai as work**. When the site has several accounts, select **Open claude.ai**, then the account. In the Pitboard window, right-click an account and select **Open claude.ai** or **Open chatgpt.com**.

    A window needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime 144.0.3719.77 or later, which Windows Update keeps up to date.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A window's title is its account's label. Links to other sites open in your default browser, and anything you connect there goes to the account your browser is signed in to. [Account windows](/reference/account-windows#account-windows) lists every place a window opens from, and [Downloads](/reference/account-windows#downloads) where it saves files.

## Sign in

The first time Pitboard opens an account's window, a note at its top names the email address to sign in with and the steps. Google's sign-in does not work: Google does not allow it inside apps, so the window stops it.

### claude.ai

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the email">
    Enter the email address the note names, then continue with email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the link on your phone">
    Open the email on your phone and tap its link. On this computer, the link would open in your browser instead of the window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    In the window, enter the code claude.ai shows on your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### chatgpt.com

Enter the account's email address, then its password or the code chatgpt.com emails you, or sign in with Microsoft or Apple.

On macOS, passkeys do not work: the system offers them to a web page only in an app it lets act as a browser. On Windows, whether they work has not been measured.

## What a window cannot do

- Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar cannot be connected, since connecting them needs Google's sign-in.
- In a claude.ai window, Google single sign-on does not work either.
- The window refuses the camera and the microphone, so voice mode does not work.
- On macOS, web notifications do not work.
- Pitboard cannot see who is signed in to a window. Its title names the account it belongs to.
- A window stays with its own account after a switch.

## Open a page from your browser

On macOS, you can send the page your browser shows to an account's window.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share the page">
    In Safari, click **Share** in the toolbar or choose **File** > **Share**. In Chrome or Firefox, choose **File** > **Share**. Then choose **Pitboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the account">
    In the **Open Link** window, select an account. Pitboard first selects the one you chose last for that site, or else the account in use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Open">
    The page opens in that account's window. If the window was already open, **Back** returns to what it showed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pitboard refuses a page on another site, and a site's sign-in link, with **Can't Open This Link**. [Refused links](/reference/account-windows#refused-links) lists every case.

With no account on the page's site, the **Open Link** window shows **No claude.ai Account**. Click **Add Account**, and the link waits there until you choose the account you added.

If **Pitboard** is not in the Share menu, see [Pitboard is missing from the Share menu](/troubleshooting/account-windows#pitboard-is-missing-from-the-share-menu).

## Sign a window out

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    Bring the account's window to the front, choose **File** > **Remove Website Data**, then click **Remove**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    In the account's window, select **Remove website data**, then **Remove**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The window starts again at the site's home page, with the sign-in note. The site is not told, so the account stays signed in on your other devices and browsers.

[Forgetting the account](/guides/accounts#forget-an-account) deletes its window's data too.
